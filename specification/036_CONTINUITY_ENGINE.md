# AI Film OS

# Continuity Engine

---

# Purpose

The Continuity Engine ensures production consistency across every shot, scene, sequence, and final edit.

It prevents continuity errors before assets are approved.

---

# Objectives

Maintain consistency for:

- Characters
- Locations
- Lighting
- Camera
- Props
- Costume
- Time
- Weather
- Story Timeline

---

# Continuity Workflow


# Continuity Score

Every production asset receives:

- Character Score
- Camera Score
- Lighting Score
- Story Score
- Technical Score

Overall Score

0–100

---

# AI Actions

If score is below threshold:

- Generate Warning
- Explain Issue
- Recommend Fix
- Optional Re-render
