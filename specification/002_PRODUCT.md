# Product

---

# Purpose

This document defines the overall product strategy, objectives, target users, major capabilities, and product scope of AI Film OS Version 1.0.

It establishes what AI Film OS is designed to accomplish and the value it delivers to creators and production teams.

---

# Product Overview

AI Film OS is a professional AI-powered production platform that manages the complete filmmaking workflow from concept development to final distribution.

Rather than acting as a single AI tool, AI Film OS coordinates multiple production processes through one unified operating system.

---

# Product Objectives

Version 1.0 focuses on building a stable production foundation.

Primary objectives include:

- Standardized production workflow
- Professional filmmaking guidance
- AI-assisted decision making
- Project knowledge management
- Production consistency
- Multi-model AI orchestration
- Asset reusability
- Team collaboration support

# Product Principles

Every feature should satisfy at least one of the following principles.

- Improve production quality
- Reduce production time
- Increase creative consistency
- Preserve project knowledge
- Reduce repetitive work
- Support professional workflows

---

# Functional Goals

The platform should enable creators to:

- Plan productions
- Organize production assets
- Generate production-ready prompts
- Maintain continuity
- Export complete production packages

---

# Non-Goals

Version 1 will NOT include:

- Real-time collaborative editing
- Built-in video rendering engine
- Built-in 3D engine
- Marketplace
- Plugin SDK
- Mobile Application
- Enterprise Administration Console

These items belong to future versions.

---

# Success Metrics

Version 1 is considered successful when:

- Production planning time is significantly reduced.
- Project continuity is maintained.
- AI recommendations follow professional standards.
- Users can complete a project using one integrated workflow.
