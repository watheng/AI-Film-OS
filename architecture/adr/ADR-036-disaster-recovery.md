# ADR-036 — Disaster Recovery

## Decision

The platform shall support disaster recovery procedures.

## Objectives

- Data Recovery
- Service Recovery
- Backup Verification

## Recovery Targets

- Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO)

## Status

Accepted
