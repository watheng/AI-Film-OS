# AI Film OS

# Professional Knowledge Engine (PKE)

---

# Purpose

The Professional Knowledge Engine (PKE) is the intelligence layer of AI Film OS.

Its purpose is to ensure that every recommendation, analysis, and generated result is based on professional filmmaking knowledge rather than generic AI responses.

PKE provides expert reasoning before content generation.

---

# Objectives

The Professional Knowledge Engine is responsible for:

- Professional analysis
- Decision support
- Best practice recommendations
- Creative guidance
- Technical validation
- Educational explanations
- Production consistency

---

# Philosophy

AI should not simply answer.

AI should think like a professional production team.

Every recommendation should include:

- What
- Why
- How
- Alternatives
- Best Practice

# Knowledge Workflow

Every AI task follows the same sequence.

```
User Request

↓

Project Memory

↓

Project Bible

↓

Professional Knowledge Engine

↓

Professional Analysis

↓

Recommendation

↓

AI Production Council

↓

Generation
```

---

# Recommendation Format

Every recommendation should contain:

## Analysis

Professional evaluation.

---

## Reason

Why this recommendation is appropriate.

---

## Alternative

Other possible solutions.

---

## Best Practice

Industry-standard approach.

---

## Warning

Potential risks or limitations.

---

# Educational Mode

When enabled, the system explains professional concepts during production.

Examples

- Why a specific lens is recommended.
- Why a lighting setup improves emotion.
- Why a story structure needs revision.

The goal is to help users improve their filmmaking knowledge while using AI Film OS.

---

# Design Principles

The Professional Knowledge Engine follows these principles.

- Explain before Generate
- Professional before Generic
- Story before Technology
- Knowledge before Automation
- Human Approval Required
