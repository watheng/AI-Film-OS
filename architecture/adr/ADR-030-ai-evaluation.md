# ADR-030 — AI Evaluation Framework

## Decision

Every AI-generated output shall be evaluated before final approval.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Creativity
- Quality
- Safety
- Cost Efficiency

## Evaluation Methods

- Automated Review
- AI Reviewer
- Human Approval

## Status

Accepted
