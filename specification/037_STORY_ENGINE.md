# AI Film OS

# Story Engine

---

# Purpose

The Story Engine is responsible for designing, developing, validating, and evolving narrative structures throughout the entire production lifecycle.

It functions as the narrative intelligence of AI Film OS.

---

# Objectives

The Story Engine provides:

- Story Generation
- Story Analysis
- Story Validation
- Narrative Consistency
- Emotional Pacing
- Character Arc Management
- Theme Preservation

# Narrative Intelligence

The Story Engine continuously evaluates:

- Plot Holes
- Character Motivation
- Emotional Flow
- Narrative Pace
