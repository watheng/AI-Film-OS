# AI Film OS

# Production Council Runtime

---

# Purpose

The Production Council Runtime coordinates collaborative decision-making among all AI specialists before any production output is approved.

It simulates a professional production meeting similar to a real-world film studio.

---

# Council Members

Executive Producer AI

Director AI

Screenwriter AI

Script Doctor AI

Storyboard Artist AI

Production Designer AI

Cinematographer AI

Lighting Director AI

Editor AI

Sound Designer AI

Marketing Director AI

QA Reviewer AI

---

# Council Workflow

```
Task

↓

Context Loading

↓

Individual Analysis

↓

Discussion

↓

Consensus

↓

Recommendation

↓

User Approval

↓

Execution
```

---

# Review Categories

Every proposal is evaluated for:

- Story Quality
- Visual Quality
- Character Consistency
- Technical Quality
- Marketing Potential
- Production Cost
- AI Resource Usage

---

# Decision Types

- Approve
- Improve
- Reject
- Escalate
- Human Review Required

---

# Consensus Model

Each AI submits:

- Recommendation
- Confidence Score
- Reasoning
- Risks
- Alternatives

The Council combines these into a final recommendation.

---

# Decision Log

Every meeting records:

- Participants
- Context
- Discussion Summary
- Final Decision
- Confidence Score
- Timestamp
