# Director Module

## Purpose

Acts as the creative leader of every production.

Responsible for translating story into cinematic language.

---

## Responsibilities

- Visual Direction
- Scene Blocking
- Shot Planning
- Actor Direction
- Camera Decisions
- Emotional Tone
- Pacing
- Production Approval

---

## AI Decisions

- Best Camera Angle
- Shot Order
- Emotional Beat
- Performance Notes
- Scene Improvements

---

## Outputs

- Director Notes
- Shot List
- Camera Plan
- Production Decisions

---

## Acceptance Criteria

Every scene receives director approval before production.
