# Character DNA Engine

---

# Purpose

The Character DNA Engine preserves complete character identity across every scene, image, video, and production asset.

Its goal is to maintain perfect character consistency regardless of AI model or production stage.

---

# Character DNA

Each character contains immutable identity data.

Examples:

- Face Structure
- Age
- Gender
- Height
- Body Shape
- Skin Tone
- Hair
- Eyes
- Voice
- Expressions
- Personality
- Clothing
- Accessories
- Color Palette
- Motion Style

- Emotion
