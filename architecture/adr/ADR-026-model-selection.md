# ADR-026 — AI Model Selection Strategy

## Decision

Select AI models dynamically based on workload.

## Priority

1. Local AI
2. Lowest Cost
3. Fastest Response
4. Highest Quality

## Supported Providers

- Local Models
- OpenAI
- Gemini
- Anthropic

Accepted
