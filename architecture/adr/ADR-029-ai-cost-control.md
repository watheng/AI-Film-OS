# ADR-029 — AI Cost Control

## Decision

AI Film OS shall monitor and optimize AI usage costs.

## Features

- Cost Dashboard
- Budget Limits
- Token Tracking
- Provider Comparison
- Cost Reports

## Goals

- Prevent unexpected costs
- Optimize provider usage
- Improve operational efficiency

## Status

Accepted
