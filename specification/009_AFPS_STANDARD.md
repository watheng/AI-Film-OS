# AI Film OS

# AI Film Production Standard (AFPS)

---

# Purpose

The AI Film Production Standard (AFPS) defines the minimum production quality requirements for every workflow, module, AI recommendation, prompt, and generated asset inside AI Film OS.

Every component of the platform must comply with these standards.

---

# Objectives

AFPS ensures:

- Professional quality
- Production consistency
- Standardized workflow
- Reusable assets
- Explainable AI decisions
- Reliable project management

---

# Core Principles

Every production should be:

- Professional
- Consistent
- Explainable
- Reproducible
- Modular
- Reviewable
- Versioned

---

# AFPS-001

## Story Standard

Requirements

- Clear premise
- Defined genre
- Theme
- Conflict
- Character goals
- Logical progression
- Meaningful ending

---

# AFPS-002

## Character Standard

Requirements

- Unique identity
- Consistent appearance
- Defined personality
- Clear motivation
- Stable costume
- Voice profile
- Reference images

- World rules
- Geography
- Culture
- Timeline

# AFPS-010

## Continuity Standard

Requirements

Maintain consistency for:

- Characters
- Locations
- Props
- Costume
- Lighting
- Camera
- Timeline
- Story progression

---

# AFPS-011

## Review Standard

Every major production stage requires review.

Mandatory review points include:

- Story
- Character
- Storyboard
- Prompt
- Image
- Video
- Final Production

---

# AFPS-012

## Output Standard

Every exported production package should include:

- Organized assets
- Version information
- Production metadata
- Export logs
- Project references

---

# Quality Levels

AI Film OS supports three production quality levels.

## Standard

Suitable for daily content creation.

---

## Professional

Suitable for commercial productions.

---

## Studio

Highest production quality.

Requires complete workflow validation and AI Production Council review.

---

# Compliance

Every module must comply with AFPS before release.

Any module that does not satisfy the required standards should be flagged for revision.
